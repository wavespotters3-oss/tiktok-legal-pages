Privacy Policy

This application is a student engineering project developed in cooperation with the Antenna Design Student Research Group at Warsaw University of Technology.

Data processed by the application

The application may process TikTok authorization tokens required to upload or publish content to the connected TikTok account. It may also process satellite image files generated by the ground station.

How the data is used

Data is used only to upload or publish selected satellite images to TikTok and to maintain the technical operation of the application.

Image hosting

Satellite images may be stored in Cloudflare R2 or another configured storage provider and made available through public URLs so that TikTok can retrieve them.

Data sharing

The application does not sell personal data. Images are shared with TikTok only for the purpose of publishing content selected for publication.

User control

Users can revoke the application's access through TikTok account settings or by contacting the project maintainer.
